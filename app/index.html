Search tips

  • Type words to search books and their pictures. Results are ranked by relevance and must contain every word. Small typos are tolerated (parqet), and word variants match each other (partitioned → partitioning).
  • Pick a suggestion from the list. Entries marked Group search a whole notion at once: Data quality also covers data quality checks, AWS Glue Data Quality and more. A bar below then lets you narrow to one keyword, or widen a keyword to its group.
  • Use "quotes" for an exact phrase: "data engineer" won't match "data engineering".
  • Pictures view: exact matches are labelled with the keyword they matched. Include close matches adds ≈ close ones (typos, word variants), which also appear automatically when nothing matches exactly.
  • Combine with the filters on the left. Remove the search with the × on its chip above the results.
